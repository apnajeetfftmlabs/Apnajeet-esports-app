Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what information the ApnaJeet app and website ("ApnaJeet", "we", "us") collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: such as your name, email address or phone number when you sign in.
  • Gameplay details: your in-game ID and name, tournament entries and results.
  • Submitted proof: screenshots you upload to verify tournament participation.
  • Reward activity: rewards and redeem codes issued to your account.
  • Device and usage data: device model, OS version, app version, crash logs and basic usage analytics.

2. How we use information

  • To run tournaments, verify submissions and deliver rewards.
  • To operate, secure and improve the app, and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To send service messages such as tournament and reward updates.
  • To respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run the app (for example hosting, authentication, analytics and crash reporting), when required by law, or to protect our users and platform.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. You can request deletion of your account and data by emailing YOUR-EMAIL@example.com from your registered email. We will respond within a reasonable time.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Children

ApnaJeet is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Some features may have higher age requirements, as stated in the app and our Terms. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices

You can access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, by contacting us. You can also manage app permissions and notifications in your device settings.

8. Third-party services

The app may use third-party services such as Google Play Services and Firebase. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions? Email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.
