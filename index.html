What is ApnaJeet?

ApnaJeet is an esports and gaming platform where users can explore tournament information, gaming tasks and reward-related features.

How do I join a tournament?

Check the available tournament information in the app, review eligibility and follow the event's registration instructions and rules.

Can I receive rewards through tasks?

Certain tasks may offer rewards according to their published conditions. Availability, eligibility and completion requirements apply. Check the details inside the app.

Are rewards or redeem codes guaranteed?

No. Rewards and redeem codes depend on the specific offer, eligibility, availability and applicable terms. Check official announcements for details.
